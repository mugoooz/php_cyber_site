About the Hub

Founded by students, for students — bridging the gap between classroom theory and the skills the industry actually hires for.

Our story

Why we exist

Kenya faces millions of attempted cyber attacks every year, yet the pipeline of trained defenders remains small. The Cybersecurity Skills Hub was started to give learners a practical path into the field: structured labs, mentorship and a community that keeps you accountable.

Mission

Make practical cybersecurity education accessible to every student in East Africa.

Vision

A regional workforce that can detect, respond to and prevent cyber threats locally.

Values

Ethics first, hands-on always, community over competition.
