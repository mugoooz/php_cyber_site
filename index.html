About our training model

Our programmes run in the evenings and on weekends so that university students can attend without missing classes. Each cohort spends 60% of its time in practical labs, 25% in guided theory and 15% on a capstone project presented to industry mentors. Graduates leave with a portfolio they can show to employers such as SOC providers, banks and telcos.
